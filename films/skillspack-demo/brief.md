# SkillsPack demo

## Film
One sentence: SkillsPack: see, switch, and fix every skill for Claude Code, Codex, and pi from your menu bar.
Audience: developers on X and LinkedIn who use coding agents.
Duration: 20–25 s
Formats: wide 16:9 (1920×1080)

## Facts (never invented)
Product: SkillsPack, a macOS menu bar app. Claim: one place to see, switch, and fix agent skills for Claude Code, Codex, and pi.
Approved copy: end card only. "SkillsPack" and "Skills for Claude Code, Codex, and pi. In your menu bar."
Numbers: only what the real app shows for the demo data (skill counts, uses, tokens). No numbers added by the film.

## Visual rules
Keep: it must look like a real screen recording of a Mac. Real UI pixels, real cursor speed, real click timing.
Avoid: centred text on a gradient, everything fading in, logo pop, particles, lens flares, captions, fake UI.
Never invent product screens, logos, or metrics.

## Sound
Real UI sounds only: trackpad clicks and switch toggles, quiet. No music, no whooshes.

## Engine
Canvas (bundled): the film is UI motion from real screen renders, under 30 s, and every frame stays seekable.

## Deliverables
Shot list, contact sheets, one video and one poster (wide), source, asset list, review note.
